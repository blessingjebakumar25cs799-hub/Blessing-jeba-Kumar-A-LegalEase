# Phase 1 – Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project
**LegalEase – AI-Powered Legal Document Generator**

## Problem Identification
Preparing common legal documents manually can be time-consuming and can require familiarity with legal language and document structure.

## Proposed Idea
LegalEase uses Generative AI to assist users in creating legal-document drafts from information supplied by the user.

## Target Users
- Individuals
- Freelancers
- Entrepreneurs
- Professionals

## Example Documents
- Non-Disclosure Agreement
- Employment Agreement
- Rental Agreement
- Service Agreement
- Partnership Agreement
- Sale Agreement
- Loan Agreement
- Privacy Agreement
- Terms and Conditions

## Expected Benefits
- Simplify document creation
- Reduce preparation time
- Provide an editable generated document
- Support TXT, DOCX and PDF output

## Outcome
The selected solution was an AI-powered legal document generator with a simple user interface and downloadable document formats.
